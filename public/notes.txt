Pharmacology Study Notes
========================

1. Antiretroviral Drugs
- NRTIs (Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitors): Tenofovir, Lamivudine, Emtricitabine
- NNRTIs (Non-Nucleoside Reverse Transcriptase Inhibitors): Efavirenz, Nevirapine, Rilpivirine
- Protease Inhibitors: Atazanavir, Darunavir, Lopinavir
- Integrase Inhibitors: Dolutegravir, Raltegravir, Bictegravir

2. Antihypertensive Drugs
- ACE Inhibitors: Enalapril, Lisinopril, Ramipril
- ARBs: Losartan, Valsartan, Olmesartan
- Calcium Channel Blockers: Amlodipine, Nifedipine, Diltiazem
- Beta Blockers: Metoprolol, Propranolol, Atenolol
- Diuretics: Hydrochlorothiazide, Furosemide, Spironolactone

3. Corticosteroids
- Glucocorticoids: Prednisolone, Dexamethasone, Hydrocortisone
- Mineralocorticoid Antagonists: Spironolactone, Eplerenone
- Glucocorticoid Antagonists/Synthesis Inhibitors: Metyrapone, Aminoglutethimide, Ketoconazole, Mifepristone

4. Antibiotics
- Penicillins: Amoxicillin, Ampicillin, Piperacillin
- Cephalosporins: Ceftriaxone, Cefixime, Cefuroxime
- Macrolides: Azithromycin, Clarithromycin, Erythromycin
- Fluoroquinolones: Ciprofloxacin, Levofloxacin, Moxifloxacin

5. Analgesics
- NSAIDs: Ibuprofen, Diclofenac, Naproxen
- Opioids: Morphine, Fentanyl, Tramadol
- Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)
